About Me

I am an interdisciplinary computational scientist, I focus on designing scalable tensor engines, which serve as the mathematical foundation for both next-generation quantum simulations and modern AI/Large Language Model (LLM) architectures. Currently a postdoctoral researcher in computer science at IRIT (CNRS, Toulouse), where I scale a GPU-based tensor engine for structured sparsity to exascale using StarPU. Previously, at the CNRS Laboratoire de Chimie et Physique Quantiques, I architected a tensor toolchain for an Advanced ERC grant — automatic optimization of tensor expressions and distributed memory tensor contractions on GPUs with excellent weak scaling. I was recently awarded the Marie Curie Fellowship Seal of Excellence (92% score) for architecting a novel HPC tensor library utilizing theory of representations and graph theory.
